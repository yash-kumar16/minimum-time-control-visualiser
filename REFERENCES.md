# References for the minimum-time visualiser

The links below are to external publications, not included copies of copyrighted text.

1. **D. Liberzon (2012).** *Calculus of Variations and Optimal Control Theory: A Concise Introduction*. Princeton University Press. [Publisher](https://press.princeton.edu/books/ebook/9781400842643/calculus-of-variations-and-optimal-control-theory-pdf) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400842643). Background for Pontryagin's maximum principle, controllability and time-optimal control.
2. **L. S. Pontryagin, V. G. Boltyanskii, R. V. Gamkrelidze and E. F. Mishchenko (1962).** *The Mathematical Theory of Optimal Processes*. Interscience. Foundational theory of optimal processes; see a university library for the edition.
3. **H. J. Sussmann (1987).** *A general theorem on local controllability*. SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization, 25(1):158–194. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0325011). General controllability context, not a proof of the displayed numerical algorithm.

**Important distinction:** these are conceptual references; the source does not claim to reproduce a published tested numerical method for solving arbitrary minimum-time problems.
